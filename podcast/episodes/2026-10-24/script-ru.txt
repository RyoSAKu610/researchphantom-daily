Первая статья: «Causal inference in staggered adoption panels: The correct comparison set depends on your intervention». Авторы: Ian Lundberg, Yaling Xu. A powerful data structure for causal inference is staggered adoption: many units are observed over many time periods, during which some units adopt an irreversible treatment at various time points while others remain untreated. Popular methods that apply in this setting include difference in difference, fixed effects, matching, and synthetic control.

Вторая статья: «PriMa-Causa: Causal Prescriptive Maintenance». Авторы: Felix Saretzky. Automated manufacturing requires data-driven decisions under uncertainty and sparse observational records, and unplanned downtime is a dominant cost of getting them wrong. Prescriptive maintenance recommends a maintenance action against an impending failure rather than merely predicting it. Established reference architectures couple predictive models with rule- or optimisation-based recommendation logic.

Третья статья: «Estimand frameworks for causal inference in clinical research and regulatory decision making». Авторы: Miguel A. Hernán, Mats J. Stensrud, Xabier García-Albéniz и др. Causal inference for clinical and regulatory decision making requires a precise definition of the causal estimand, that is, what we want to estimate. Three frameworks are commonly used to describe causal estimands: PICO and its extensions, the target trial framework, and the E9(R1) Addendum of the International Council for Harmonisation (ICH).

Четвёртая статья: «A Semi-Analytical Inference Framework for Structural Causal Models». Авторы: Tatiana Boura, Robert Peharz. Structural Causal Models (SCMs) provide a principled framework for observational, interventional, and counterfactual reasoning, all of which rely on probabilistic inference. Existing frameworks either transform SCMs into alternative tractable representations or rely on approximate inference with expressive generative models.

Пятая статья: «A plug-in estimator of the non-iterative conditional expectation g-formula using long short-term memory models». Авторы: Jing Li, Sophia M. Rein, Zilong Wang и др. The non-iterative conditional expectation (NICE) plug-in estimator of the g-formula can be used to estimate the causal effects of sustained treatment strategies, even in the presence of treatment-confounder feedback, under the assumptions of exchangeability, positivity, and consistency. In practical applications, parametric models are commonly used to estimate the joint distribution of confounders, treatments, and outcomes over time.

Седьмая статья: «Where to weight? Estimating population causal effects with weighted double score matching in complex surveys». Авторы: Yukang Zeng, Guangyu Tong, Jiaqi Tong и др. Double score matching is an emerging method for causal inference that combines propensity and prognostic scores to achieve dimension reduction and model robustness. However, when the observational data are drawn from a complex survey, the existing literature does not provide sufficient guidance on the proper use of survey weights in matching strategies for population-level causal inference.

Восьмая статья: «Estimating Causal Bounds from Observational Data With Neural Generative Models». Авторы: Max Zhu, Srivathsan Amruth, Sina Akbari и др. Identifying causal bounds from discrete observational data is a fundamental problem in causal inference. While exact analytical bounds exist for certain graphs, this is not generally the case. In this paper, we introduce a framework for estimating such bounds using neural generative models together with a new extrapolation procedure to estimate these bounds more accurately.
