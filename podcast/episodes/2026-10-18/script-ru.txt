Первая статья: «Physical Canvas: A Vision Tactile World Model For Robot Policy Evaluation And Learning». Авторы: Zebin Xing, Yuhang Zheng, Yupeng Zheng и др. Evaluating and post-training robot policies through physical interaction is costly and difficult to scale, motivating action-conditioned world models as interactive simulators. However, visually plausible predictions can misrepresent contact dynamics, providing unreliable feedback for policy evaluation and learning. We introduce PhysCanvas, a state-consistent visuo-tactile world model for contact-rich robot manipulation. PhysCanvas unifies multi-view RGB, tactile images, marker displacement, task state, and actions within a unified vision space.

Recent advances in visuospatial understanding and agentic reasoning enable direct robotic manipulation with general-purpose multimodal models, but their embodied capabilities remain poorly understood. This paper systematically evaluate GPT-6 Astra for closed-loop robotic manipulation using a lightweight agent execution framework.

Третья статья: «FIT-MAC: A Closed-Loop Multi-Agent Framework for Robust Vision-Language-Action Models». Авторы: Jiatong Li, Jisheng Dang, Qiangqiang Ma и др. Vision-language-action (VLA) models have achieved strong performance on standard robotic manipulation benchmarks. However, their reliability can degrade substantially when task conditions deviate from the nominal setting. Perturbations in object positions and task instructions may lead to complete task failure. Existing VLA policies generally lack an explicit runtime mechanism for distinguishing these failure modes and selecting an appropriate recovery strategy.

Четвёртая статья: «EventSync: Event-Driven Memory Updates for History-Dependent Manipulation». Авторы: Jingjiao Zhao, Dong Jing, Mingyu Cao и др. History-dependent robotic manipulation requires task context and interaction history, as the current observation alone is insufficient to determine the next action. Hierarchical frameworks use task memory for high-level subgoal reasoning and low-level policies for execution. However, memory updates require accurate completion assessment: similar observations may correspond to different execution phases, and prematurely recording completion can mislead subsequent subgoal reasoning.

Пятая статья: «AWLS: Advantage-Weighted Latent Steering for One-Step Generative Robotic Manipulation Policies». Авторы: Jiahong Tang, Jiaju He, Zhiyuan Yang и др. Human-collected real-world robotic manipulation demonstrations often contain mixed-quality trajectories, including hesitant, indirect, or inefficient action segments. While diffusion- and flow-based policies can model such multimodal data, standard behavior cloning (BC) assigns equal likelihood to all demonstrated actions and may therefore reproduce suboptimal modes.

Шестая статья: «PLaCA: Learning Patch-Level Future Latent Change for Action Generation». Авторы: Zhan Yi, Yuchen Wang, Chao Li и др. Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models generate robot actions from visual observations and language instructions, but effective manipulation also requires anticipating how the environment will change. Existing world-action models explore future image prediction and latent dynamics modeling. However, generating complete future images can be computationally expensive, while highly compressed latent representations may obscure local changes. To address these challenges, we propose PLaCA, a world-to-action framework built around future latent change.

Седьмая статья: «HATCH: Hardware-Attested Task Chains for Verifiable Manipulation». Авторы: Shiza Charania. Capability claims in robot manipulation are unverifiable, and existing leaderboards are each closed, simulated, or gameable. We propose HATCH, a robot escape room: an openly specified, physically replicable, sensor-scored, and unrehearsable benchmark. Tasks span five skill categories at three difficulty levels and are chained so that the sensor confirming one task's completion unlocks the next, turning a room into a single long-horizon evaluation. Success is confirmed by instrumentation rather than human judgment, scoring is embodiment-agnostic, and object placements are randomized by a run-day seed.
