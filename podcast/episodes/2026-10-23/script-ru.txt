Первая статья: «Reliability-Aware Probabilistic Fusion for Multimodal Learning with Incomplete Evidence». Авторы: Patitapaban Palo, Abhisek Tiwari, Mattia Carletti и др. Multimodal inputs may be missing or degraded, raising distinct questions of evidence availability and trust. We introduce a reliability-aware Gaussian Product-of-Experts (PoE) in which an input-dependent reliability weight tempers each modality's latent precision, while missing experts are omitted. We characterize how reliability and availability affect posterior information and expert influence.

Вторая статья: «Reusing Multimodal Agents: Efficient Evaluation of Sound Designs in Games». Авторы: Thai Nguyen Van, Ibrahim Khan, Mury Fajar Dewantoro и др. This paper introduces a novel framework for efficiently evaluating in-game sound designs using multimodal learning and reinforcement learning. Sound design significantly influences gameplay and accessibility; however, objective evaluation methods, defined here as automated assessments based on agent performance rather than human ratings, often require retraining agents from scratch for each sound design.

Пятая статья: «MM-LeJEPA: Correlated Gaussian Regularization for Multimodal Joint-Embedding Predictive Architectures». Авторы: Haowen Bai, Rui Gong$, Meichen Liu и др. LeJEPA derives an isotropic Gaussian embedding target by minimizing, under its task assumptions, a Fisher information criterion that controls the task-averaged squared prediction bias. This enables unimodal self-supervised learning without reconstruction decoders, exponential-moving-average teachers, or contrastive negatives. However, its isotropic task prior does not account for the cross-modal structure of multimodal tasks that read shared content.

Шестая статья: «AdamPM: Memory-Efficient LLM Optimization via Moment Pooling». Авторы: Xiaowei Ji, Lishan Xu, Jianing An и др. Adam is widely used for training large language models (LLMs) owing to its strong optimization performance and training stability, but its element-wise estimation for the first and second moments incur substantial memory overhead. Although numerous memory-efficient optimizers have been proposed, many incur additional computational overhead or require additional tuning of hyperparameters.

Седьмая статья: «MSCR: Jointly Balancing Utilization Across Modalities and Discovering Synergistic Information». Авторы: Xinyu Chen, Liangjian Wen, Jiang Duan и др. In multimodal learning, modality imbalance often lets one modality dominate optimization and keeps the model from fully exploiting cross-modal interactions. Balance-oriented methods such as the Multimodal Competition Regularizer (MCR) perform well by alleviating this imbalance during training.

Multimodal learning commonly assumes paired or synchronized observations, yet independently collected datasets rarely provide such correspondence. We introduce CIIR, a modular encoder--decoder framework that connects heterogeneous datasets through a class-indexed address space while preserving each dataset's native input format and output module.
