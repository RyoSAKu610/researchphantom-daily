Existing vision-language models (VLMs) remain unreliable for fine-grained visual perception. In this report, we present WeVisOne-VL (WeChat Vision All-in-One), a powerful VLM that unifies object-level and pixel-level perception through structured next-token prediction.

Вторая статья: «Open-Vocabulary Models and Label Propagation for Uncertainty-aware Pseudo-Labeling of Object Detection Datasets». Авторы: Jonathan Klees, Timo Heller, Matthias Rottmann. Object detection models rely on large-scale annotated datasets that require substantial manual annotation effort. The labeling costs further increase when annotation uncertainty is captured through soft labels, as multiple annotations must be collected for each object. We address this challenge by introducing a semi-supervised framework for the uncertainty-aware annotation of object detection datasets.

Третья статья: «UniRAP: Towards Unified Part-level Physical Affordance Reasoning and Actionable Perception». Авторы: Linfei Li, Ruining Hu, Lin Zhang и др. Vision-language perception has achieved impressive progress in aligning natural language with visual observations, yet grounding high-level semantics into part-level physical interaction remains challenging. To address this gap, we propose UniRAP, a unified model for inferring part-level physical affordances and mapping language instructions to actionable geometric representations.

Object detection models for autonomous driving commonly experience substantial performance drops when deployed under adverse weather due to the domain shift between training data and real-world operating conditions. This degradation is especially evident when models trained on clear-weather images encounter foggy environments with reduced visibility and contrast.

Deep neural networks deployed on smart glasses must adapt to changes in the distribution of incoming data while adhering to strict privacy and device constraints. We present No Frames, Just Frequencies, a bandwidth-efficient source-assisted adaptation framework that characterizes distribution shifts using compact frequency descriptors computed on-device.

Шестая статья: «Guess and Send: World-Model-Guided Semantic Packet Transmission for Robust Video Perception». Авторы: Yan Gong, Zheng Chu, Wei Koong Chai и др. Reliable video perception over bandwidth-constrained wireless links requires transmitting information that the receiver cannot already recover from its evolving scene representation. This paper introduces WorldGT, a world-model-guided semantic packet transmission framework that prioritizes information according to its contribution to the receiver's perception.
