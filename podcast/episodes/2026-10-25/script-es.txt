El segundo artículo: «Open-Vocabulary Models and Label Propagation for Uncertainty-aware Pseudo-Labeling of Object Detection Datasets». Los autores son Jonathan Klees, Timo Heller, Matthias Rottmann. Object detection models rely on large-scale annotated datasets that require substantial manual annotation effort. The labeling costs further increase when annotation uncertainty is captured through soft labels, as multiple annotations must be collected for each object. We address this challenge by introducing a semi-supervised framework for the uncertainty-aware annotation of object detection datasets.

This degradation is especially evident when models trained on clear-weather images encounter foggy environments with reduced visibility and contrast.
