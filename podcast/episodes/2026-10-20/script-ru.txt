Вторая статья: «Primal-Dual Adaptive Subgradient Methods». Авторы: Karthik Prakhya, Zimeng Wang, Alp Yurtsever. We propose adaptive primal-dual subgradient methods for constrained convex optimization. Our first method applies an AdaGrad-style step-sizes to the dual problem and recovers primal solutions by averaging the associated Fenchel-type oracle outputs. It achieves convergence for the dual objective residual, primal objective residual, and feasibility gap in the nonsmooth regime, while automatically improving to when the dual objective is smooth. We further develop an accelerated variant, which retains the nonsmooth rate while achieving convergence in the smooth regime under mild assumptions. These guarantees require no prior knowledge of the underlying regularity. We demonstrate the empirical performance of the proposed methods on optimal transport and semidefinite relaxation of the Max-Cut problem.

Третья статья: «Controllability Allocation Scores for Linear Infinite-Dimensional Systems». Авторы: Daisuke Inoue, Kazuhiro Sato. We define finite-time controllability allocation scores (CASs) as optimal solutions to two convex optimization problems that allocate a fixed actuation budget among prescribed input functions of linear infinite-dimensional continuum systems with selected target outputs. The objectives are expressed using controllability Gramians for the selected outputs and correspond to maximizing the volume of output changes reachable with unit control energy or minimizing the mean energy required for unit output changes. To compute these CASs, we discretize the system in space, construct the resulting Gramians, and solve the resulting allocation problems by a domain-aware projected gradient descent method. We study the behavior of CASs when the actuator-to-target responses of the spatially discretized systems converge to those of the continuum system as the mesh is refined.
