Il secondo articolo: «Primal-Dual Adaptive Subgradient Methods». Gli autori sono Karthik Prakhya, Zimeng Wang, Alp Yurtsever. We propose adaptive primal-dual subgradient methods for constrained convex optimization. Our first method applies an AdaGrad-style step-sizes to the dual problem and recovers primal solutions by averaging the associated Fenchel-type oracle outputs. It achieves convergence for the dual objective residual, primal objective residual, and feasibility gap in the nonsmooth regime, while automatically improving to when the dual objective is smooth.

Il terzo articolo: «Controllability Allocation Scores for Linear Infinite-Dimensional Systems». Gli autori sono Daisuke Inoue, Kazuhiro Sato. We define finite-time controllability allocation scores (CASs) as optimal solutions to two convex optimization problems that allocate a fixed actuation budget among prescribed input functions of linear infinite-dimensional continuum systems with selected target outputs.

Il quinto articolo: «Universal Online Convex Optimization With Delayed Feedback». Gli autori sono Hao Qiu, Andrew Jacobsen, Emmanuel Esposito, et al. We study universal online convex optimization with delayed feedback. We develop a single algorithm that simultaneously adapts to general convex, strongly convex, and exp-concave losses without prior knowledge of the curvature class or parameters.

L'ottavo articolo: «Simulation-free approximate Bayesian computation for stochastic reaction networks». Gli autori sono Zekai Li, Mauricio Barahona, Philipp Thomas. Uncovering stochastic reaction networks from data is central to single-cell biology. Simulation-based inference has evolved into a flexible paradigm for this purpose, circumventing the computational intractability of the likelihood. Notably, approximate Bayesian computation performs this inference by carrying out extensive Gillespie simulations, usually at a high computational cost.
