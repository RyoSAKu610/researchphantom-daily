Первая статья: «Towards English - Mizo Legal Machine Translation: Corpus Creation, Analysis, and Benchmarks». Авторы: Telem Joyson Singh, Sanabam Boyshana, Sanasam Ranbir Singh и др. Indian court proceedings are held in English, so speakers of languages with limited translation technology have limited access to them. We present the first English Mizo parallel dataset of Indian court judgments. It contains 13,168 sentence pairs from the Gauhati High Court and the Supreme Court of India. Professional native Mizo translators translated complete documents; sentence pairs were manually aligned, and two independent reviewers checked the results. Our study finds that English words are borrowed in a very selective way: official and legal terms are almost always kept in English, but words for everyday things like actions, situations, and results are usually expressed in native Mizo. Mizo diacritics are also used inconsistently across the corpus.

Вторая статья: «How to Use Parallel Data for Multilingual Pretraining». Авторы: Zhihong Huang, Yao Lu, Derek F. Wong и др. High-quality human-translated parallel corpora can improve cross-lingual transfer, but are typically too small for large-scale pretraining. Machine-translated web corpora help overcome this limitation, making parallel data available at pretraining scale. Standard multilingual pretraining, however, samples source documents and their translations independently, without explicitly using their parallel structure. We investigate how to use the parallel structure of the data to improve multilingual pretraining by concatenating each document with its translation in the same training sequence. We compare this parallel pretraining (PPT) approach with standard pretraining (SPT), training models from scratch at matched token budgets.

Третья статья: «UvA-MT's Participation in the WMT26 General Translation Shared Task». Авторы: Di Wu, Sergey Troshin, Wafaa Mohammed и др. This paper presents UvA-MT's submission to the WMT 2026 General Machine Translation shared task, competing in the unconstrained track across all 23 translation directions. This year, we fully leverage the test-time methods of Large Language Models (LLMs) for machine translation. Specifically, (1) we use sequential sampling with a refinement prompt to generate a pool of translation candidates for each source segment, a strategy shown to outperform traditional parallel sampling; and (2) we employ LLMs as pairwise judges to select the best candidate via a round-robin voting mechanism, with offline evaluation on the WMT25 benchmark showing a very clear improvement over point-wise best-of-N selection. Combined, these two strategies form our WMT26 submission.

Четвёртая статья: «Hybrid Momentum Methods for Optimizing Deep Learning Models». Авторы: Haifeng Xu, Yijin Ren, Chenyu Zhou и др. Momentum is central to stochastic optimization in deep learning, including the training of large language models. Classical momentum methods typically rely on a fast exponential moving average (EMA) scheme, which discounts past gradients on a fixed timescale and may underweight older gradients that remain informative. This creates a trade-off between retaining long-term history and adapting to recent changes. We introduce Hybrid Momentum (HyM), which combines a linearly weighted average of past gradients with a geometrically discounted fast momentum. The former assigns weights proportional to the gradients' iteration indices and retains a growing span of history, while the latter emphasizes recent gradients.

Пятая статья: «DeepSEA: A Multilingual Evaluation Benchmark for Southeast Asia». Авторы: Jiuzhou Han, Abhishek Bijay Mishra, Trevor Cohn и др. While language models have demonstrated remarkable capabilities, their performance remains disproportionately skewed towards high-resource languages. This disparity is particularly acute for the 700 million inhabitants of Southeast Asia, where vast linguistic diversity remains under-represented in existing benchmarks, often suffering from limited language coverage or narrow task scopes.

Шестая статья: «Towards Figurative Language Understanding in Moroccan Darija: An Evaluation of Large Language Models». Авторы: Jihad R'baiti, Youssef Hmamouche, Emmanuele Chersoni и др. Figurative language remains a major challenge for Machine Translation (MT) systems, as the intended meaning of such expressions often goes beyond literal translation. Although recent advances in Large Language Models (LLMs) improved the translation of Arabic dialects, benchmark resources for evaluating figurative language translation in Moroccan Darija remain scarce. To this end, we introduce the Moroccan Darija Figurative Language Corpus (MDFC), a benchmark dataset for MT and multiple-choice question (MCQ) selection covering idioms, metaphors, and proverbs. Using MDFC, we evaluate 19 open- and closed-weight language models and MT systems at both the expression and contextual levels under zero-shot and six-shot settings.
