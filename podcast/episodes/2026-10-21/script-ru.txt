Первая статья: «MIDI-REPA: Improving Text-to-Music Generation with Symbolic Representation Alignment». Авторы: Song-Ze Yu, Fang-Duo Tsai, Yi-Hsuan Yang. State-of-the-art text-to-music models increasingly rely on scaling model capacity, data, and compute, while still learning musical structure directly from dense audio. Yet melody, rhythm, harmony, and structure can be represented compactly in symbolic form. We introduce MIDI-REPA, a representation-alignment framework that derives MIDI supervision from automatic music transcription (AMT) to align intermediate representations of a flow-matching DiT. Building on the 122M-parameter FluxAudio-S baseline from the 2026 ICME Academic Text-To-Music Challenge, we investigate what, where, and when to align.

Вторая статья: «MUSEBENCH: A Benchmark for AI-Generated Music Detection and Artifact Analysis». Авторы: Yi Chen Liu, Syed Irfan Ali Meerza, Jiawei Yu и др. Advances in generative AI have made it possible to produce music that is nearly indistinguishable from human compositions, raising the question of whether such works can be reliably distinguished from human compositions. We present MUSEBENCH, a large-scale benchmark for AI-generated music detection and artifact analysis, comprising over 361K tracks.

Третья статья: «MSVAE: Rethinking Musical Semantics in Continuous Latent Spaces». Авторы: Guobin Ma, Yuepeng Jiang, Dake Guo и др. Continuous latent representations underlie recent diffusion-based lyrics-to-song and text-to-music systems, yet aligning lyric conditions with these latents remains difficult: existing systems resort to generator-side machinery such as sentence-level timestamps, auxiliary alignment objectives, or semi-autoregressive decoding, while the latents they predict are still produced by autoencoders trained purely for reconstruction. We address this difficulty from the representation side.

Четвёртая статья: «TASTE: Taxonomy-Aware Symbolic Piano Generation from Textual Description». Авторы: Mingyang Yao, Jingyue Huang, Ke Chen и др. Text-conditioned symbolic music generation has attracted growing interest with the development of generative models. Yet two questions remain underexplored: what aspects of music the conditioning text should describe, and how the corresponding data should be collected and trained to improve controllability and text-music alignment. We address these challenges with TASTE, a taxonomy-aware framework for symbolic music generation with a main focus on piano pieces. First, we introduce a taxonomy of musical elements that specifies how a piece can be captured through textual description in a musically grounded way.

Пятая статья: «Harmonic Representations in Symbolic Music Transformers are Localized and Controllable». Авторы: Adi Silberschein, Megan Wei, Tamar Rott Shaham. Transformer models trained on symbolic music have become remarkably capable. However, it is yet unclear how they represent music structure, and whether understanding their internal representations can be used to unlock methods for model control. We examine this through a case study of harmonic analysis: when a music transformer navigates a cadence, what does it internally "know" about its harmonic context, and can that knowledge be used to affect the generation?

Седьмая статья: «PAG-Agent: Zero-Shot Personalized Music Album Generation via Aesthetic Profile Distillation». Авторы: Yifeng Mao, Ziye Wang, Yutong Shen и др. Large Music Models (LMMs) can synthesize high-fidelity audio and support increasingly flexible prompt control, yet modeling the subjective preferences of an individual listener remains underexplored. We present PAG-Agent, a zero-shot multimodal framework that uses an explicit Personal Aesthetic Profile to bridge user preference inference and music generation control. A multimodal LLM distills favorite-song audio and contextual user information into this structured representation, which a planning LLM translates into generation instructions for existing music synthesizers.
