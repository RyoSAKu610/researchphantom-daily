Indonesia's fragmented geography and uneven infrastructure pose persistent challenges for adopting new educational technologies. While national initiatives aim to mainstream AI in K-12 schools by 2025, policy readiness does not automatically mean teachers are ready for the classroom. This study surveys Indonesian K-12 teachers to examine: (1) their understanding of AI, (2) how they and their students use AI, and (3) their views on the acceptability of AI in learning activities.

두 번째 논문: "Comparing Computer Use Behavior Across Multiple Levels of Abstraction". 저자: Ananya Gubbi Mohanbabu, Rosiana Natalie, Anhong Guo 외. Interaction traces record how people and agents use computers, and comparing them can reveal usability barriers, differences in expertise, and human--agent similarity. Yet traces are recorded as low-level, interface-specific actions, making comparisons difficult across tasks, interfaces, and input modalities. Existing approaches either compare these actions directly, reduce traces to aggregate outcomes, or summarize them at higher-level goals, obscuring where behavior differs.

Current Virtual Reality systems typically use cameras to capture user input from controllers or free-hand mid-air interaction. In this paper, we argue that this is a key impediment to productivity scenarios in VR, which require continued interaction over prolonged periods of time---a requirement that controller or free-hand input in mid-air does not satisfy.

Large language models (LLMs) are increasingly used as user simulators, but can they faithfully reproduce an individual's behavior and decision logic over time? We investigate this question in a longitudinal paper-trading study with 80 participants.
