第一篇论文：「AI Misconception and Adoption Among Indonesian K-12 Teachers」。作者是Alham Fikri Aji、Afifa Amriani、Rendi Chevi等。Indonesia's fragmented geography and uneven infrastructure pose persistent challenges for adopting new educational technologies. While national initiatives aim to mainstream AI in K-12 schools by 2025, policy readiness does not automatically mean teachers are ready for the classroom. This study surveys Indonesian K-12 teachers to examine: (1) their understanding of AI, (2) how they and their students use AI, and (3) their views on the acceptability of AI in learning activities. Through semi-automated thematic analysis, we identify common misconceptions and use cases.

第二篇论文：「Comparing Computer Use Behavior Across Multiple Levels of Abstraction」。作者是Ananya Gubbi Mohanbabu、Rosiana Natalie、Anhong Guo等。Interaction traces record how people and agents use computers, and comparing them can reveal usability barriers, differences in expertise, and human--agent similarity. Yet traces are recorded as low-level, interface-specific actions, making comparisons difficult across tasks, interfaces, and input modalities. Existing approaches either compare these actions directly, reduce traces to aggregate outcomes, or summarize them at higher-level goals, obscuring where behavior differs. We present MAP (Multi-level Action Parsing), a hierarchical semantic representation and annotation pipeline that maps traces into a common vocabulary across linked levels of abstraction.

第三篇论文：「TapID: Rapid touch interaction in virtual reality using wearable sensing」。作者是Manuel Meier、Paul Streli、Andreas Fender等。Current Virtual Reality systems typically use cameras to capture user input from controllers or free-hand mid-air interaction. In this paper, we argue that this is a key impediment to productivity scenarios in VR, which require continued interaction over prolonged periods of time---a requirement that controller or free-hand input in mid-air does not satisfy. To address this challenge, we bring rapid touch interaction on surfaces to Virtual Reality---the input modality that users have grown used to on phones and tablets for continued use. We present TapID, a wrist-based inertial sensing system that complements headset-tracked hand poses to trigger input in VR.

第四篇论文：「Small Average Error, Wide Individual Error: General-Purpose LLMs Estimating Energy Intake from Meal Photos in a Feeding Trial」。作者是Xiaomeng Li。Photo-based estimation of food energy could ease dietary assessment, and general-purpose multimodal large language models (LLMs) make such estimates easy to obtain. Most evaluations use one photo taken before eating and compare with energy served, while dietary assessment needs energy eaten. We evaluated six models from two vendors (Claude Haiku, Sonnet and Opus 5.5; GPT-6 luna, 6.1 sol and 6 astra) on 802 meals from ACETADA, a controlled feeding trial with weighed intake, with the before photo only, before and after photos in separate calls (subtraction), or both photos in one request. Three results ran against intuition.

第五篇论文：「Beyond Behavioral Accuracy: Evaluating Individual-Level Fidelity in LLM Investor Simulation」。作者是Jiajie He、Yuanhong Jiang、Xintong Chen等。Large language models (LLMs) are increasingly used as user simulators, but can they faithfully reproduce an individual's behavior and decision logic over time? We investigate this question in a longitudinal paper-trading study with 80 participants. Using rolling next-day predictions, we evaluate trading activity, buy-sell structure, asset selection, and portfolio outcomes, complemented by controlled input ablations and participant-rated explanation alignment. No evaluated LLM reliably outperforms a simple recent-activity persistence baseline for predicting trade occurrence, while buy-sell structure and asset selection remain difficult to reproduce.
